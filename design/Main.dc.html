Not a loan — what does that mean?

A Fundo advance is a purchase of part of your future business income. You get money now and pay it back from your deposits, in fixed payments on the schedule you choose.

There’s no interest rate: the cost is the fixed amount shown on your quote. Because it’s for business use, it’s meant for money you earn from your work — not wages from an employer or benefits.

If a payment can’t go through, we’ll text you before we try again, and you can call us to move a payment.
